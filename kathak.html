Kathak
NORTH INDIA

The Art of Storytelling and Rhythm

Kathak is one of India's major classical dance traditions. It is known for graceful movements, rhythmic footwork, expressive storytelling and elegant spins.

The word Kathak is connected with the tradition of storytelling. The dance combines rhythm, music, expressions and movement to present stories and emotions through performance.
