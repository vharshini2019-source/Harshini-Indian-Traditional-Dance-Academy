ABOUT SATTRIYA

The Sacred Dance Tradition of Assam

Sattriya is one of the eight major classical dance traditions of India. It originated in Assam and was developed as part of the Vaishnavite monastic tradition known as the Sattras.

The dance combines graceful movements, expressive gestures, devotional themes and dramatic storytelling. Traditionally, Sattriya was performed by monks in monasteries as a form of spiritual and artistic expression.

Today, Sattriya is performed on national and international stages and continues to preserve the rich cultural heritage of Assam.

HISTORY & HERITAGE

Origins of Sattriya

Sattriya developed through the devotional movement of Assam and became an important part of the region's cultural identity.

01

Founder

Sattriya is traditionally associated with Srimanta Sankardeva, the great Vaishnavite saint, scholar and cultural reformer of Assam.

02

Origin

The dance originated in the Sattras, the Vaishnavite monasteries established in Assam.

03

Tradition

Sattriya traditionally presents stories and teachings connected with Lord Krishna and Vaishnavite devotion.

ELEMENTS OF SATTRIYA

Beauty, Expression & Devotion

Abhinaya

Facial expressions and gestures are used to communicate emotions and devotional stories.

Hand Gestures

Traditional hand gestures play an important role in expressing characters, actions and emotions.

Rhythm

Rhythmic movements are performed with traditional musical accompaniment and percussion.

Storytelling

Mythological and devotional stories are presented through expressive dance and dramatic movements.

COSTUME & MUSIC

The Traditional Beauty of Sattriya

Sattriya costumes are traditionally inspired by the cultural heritage of Assam. Performers wear elegant traditional garments and ornaments appropriate to the character and performance.

The dance is accompanied by traditional Assamese musical instruments and devotional compositions. The khol, flute and cymbals are important elements of Sattriya performances.

Together, music, costume, rhythm and expression create the distinctive visual and spiritual character of Sattriya.
