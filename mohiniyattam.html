Mohiniyattam

Mohiniyattam
KERALA, INDIA

The Dance of Grace and Elegance

Mohiniyattam is a classical dance tradition from Kerala, known for its graceful, gentle and flowing movements. The dance is recognised for its elegance and expressive storytelling.

Mohiniyattam combines rhythm, music, facial expressions and delicate movements to present emotions and stories through a unique classical dance style.
