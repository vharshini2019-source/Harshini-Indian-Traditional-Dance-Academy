About Our Academy

OUR STORY

Preserving India's Cultural Heritage Through Dance

Harshini Indian Traditional Dance Academy is a platform dedicated to celebrating the richness, diversity and beauty of Indian dance traditions.

India is home to a wide variety of classical, folk and regional dance forms. Each dance represents the history, culture, music, traditions and identity of its people.

Our academy aims to bring these beautiful dance traditions together in one platform where students can explore, learn and appreciate the cultural diversity of India.

OUR PURPOSE

Mission & Vision

🎯

Our Mission

To promote and preserve India's traditional dance heritage by creating an engaging platform for students to learn about classical, folk and state-wise dance forms.

👁️

Our Vision

To become a digital platform that connects young learners with India's rich cultural traditions and inspires them to appreciate and celebrate the diversity of Indian dance.
